# Production Workflow

## Preferred AI-generated content sources

1. Original text-to-video generations using your own prompts.
2. Original virtual-host/commentary videos created from your own scripts.
3. Abstract/satisfying loops with no real people, no copyrighted characters, and no misleading realism.
4. AI-assisted videos based on original footage you record.
5. Licensed/authorized assets only when you have a clear rights log.

## Daily automated workflow

1. Pick one channel/account.
2. Run `ai-chain` with a query or trend direction.
3. AI#1 Trend Scout extracts trend patterns through YouTube API or your notes.
4. AI#2 Prompt Engineer creates an original concept.
5. AI#3 Video Generator creates a prompt packet for ComfyUI/Wan/LTX/Hunyuan.
6. AI#4 Video Reviewer checks monetization/TOS risk.
7. AI#5 Channel Router picks a configured channel or sends to manual review.
8. Generate the video locally through ComfyUI.
9. Upload private.
10. Review in YouTube Studio.
11. Publish manually.
12. Track views, retention, subscribers, and revenue when available.

## Minimum success requirements

- Start with 1-3 Shorts per day total until quality is proven.
- Strong first 1.5 seconds.
- Clear niche identity per account.
- Original concept and prompt for each video.
- Avoid near-identical mass-produced templates.
- Keep a generation log for every clip.
- Use private upload checks before public release.
- Disclose realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered content where required.

## Safer starting niches

1. AI oddly satisfying / abstract loops.
2. Fictional cute dog animations.
3. Original virtual-host commentary.
4. EVE/gaming educational shorts with original commentary.

## High-risk niches

- Celebrity/real-person skits.
- Fake realistic news/disaster clips.
- Copyrighted characters or franchise lookalikes.
- No-commentary VTuber clip dumps.
- Mass-generated template videos with only small wording changes.
